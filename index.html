Acrilus

Acrilus is an open architecture and standards framework for personal digital information management and security.

Framework Security Education Research Product

Overview

Modern digital life spans thousands of files, accounts, devices, records, and recovery pathways. Acrilus explores how these systems can be organized, secured, and maintained using structured information architecture and standardized personal infrastructure practices.

The project publishes architecture documents, security guides, research artifacts, and educational materials while exploring a future metadata-driven Personal Information Management System (PIMS).

Focus Areas

  • Personal information architecture
  • Digital identity segmentation
  • Credential and recovery security
  • Long-term digital resilience
  • Personal information management systems

Project Materials

  • Framework and architecture documentation
  • Personal digital security architecture
  • Research and conceptual models
  • Educational curriculum
  • Product architecture and roadmap
