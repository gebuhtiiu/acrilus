# Digital Life Skills Curriculum — K–12 Progression

## Guiding philosophy

Digital life skills should be taught progressively, just like reading, writing, and mathematics.

## Stage 1 — Early Elementary (Grades 1–3)
Focus: basic digital habits, what files are, folders, naming, private information, password basics, device care.

## Stage 2 — Upper Elementary (Grades 4–6)
Focus: structured organization, clearer names, simple backups, personal-vs-public information, stronger password ideas.

## Stage 3 — Middle School (Grades 7–9)
Focus: metadata concepts, phishing awareness, password managers, search and retrieval, digital safety.

## Stage 4 — High School (Grades 10–12)
Focus: personal information systems, lifecycle management, versioning concepts, segmentation, archiving.

## Stage 5 — Adult / Professional
Focus: identity architecture, credential systems, recoverability, governance, long-term digital resilience.
