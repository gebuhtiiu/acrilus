# The Acrilus Manifesto

Digital information is one of the defining artifacts of modern life.

Every individual now generates large collections of documents, accounts, photos, records, and dependencies, yet society provides almost no structured guidance for managing them. Important files are often not lost because they were deleted, but because they can no longer be found. Recovery paths fail not only because of hacks, but because nobody designed them.

Acrilus exists to address that gap.

The project seeks to turn digital organization from an improvised habit into a structured discipline. Its goal is not organization for its own sake, but clarity, accessibility, security, and long-term maintainability.

A digital life should be designed intentionally.
