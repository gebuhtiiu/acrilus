# Positioning and Startup Narrative

## Core positioning

Acrilus sits at the intersection of:
- Personal Information Management
- Digital Life Infrastructure
- Personal Digital Security

## Short description

Acrilus is a framework and future platform for designing, organizing, and securing personal digital systems.

## Realistic public scope

### Layer 1 — Framework
Standards, guides, and methodology.

### Layer 2 — Toolkit
Worksheets, templates, and small utilities.

### Layer 3 — Platform
A future metadata-driven PIMS that sits on top of existing storage systems.

## Why the narrative works

It is ambitious but credible. It presents Acrilus as infrastructure-first, not hype-first.
