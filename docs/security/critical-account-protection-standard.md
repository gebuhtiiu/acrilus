# Critical Account Protection Standard

## Purpose

Critical accounts are accounts whose compromise would materially threaten identity, finances, communications, or access to many downstream systems.

## Typical critical accounts

- primary email
- password manager
- banking and core finance
- primary cloud-storage owner account
- phone carrier
- recovery-email hubs

## Minimum controls

- unique strong password
- strong MFA
- documented recovery path
- backup codes stored safely
- periodic review

## Review cadence

At least quarterly, confirm that:
- the recovery email is still valid
- the recovery phone is still valid
- MFA is still active and under your control
- backup codes are stored and accessible
