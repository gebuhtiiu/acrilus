# Product Vision

## Working concept

Acrilus is envisioned as a metadata-driven Personal Information Management System (PIMS) that sits on top of existing storage systems rather than forcing users to migrate all files into a proprietary silo.

## Product thesis

There is a gap between note-taking systems and structured data systems. Acrilus aims to bridge that gap by combining:
- file-centric information management
- metadata-driven retrieval
- lifecycle awareness
- digital governance principles
- security-conscious architecture

## Long-term objective

Enable individuals to organize, retrieve, secure, and maintain complex digital records with the same rigor commonly seen in professional information systems.
