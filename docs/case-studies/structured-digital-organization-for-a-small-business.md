# Case Study — Structured Digital Organization for a Small Business

## Initial situation

A small business stored contracts, invoices, operational files, and marketing materials across email, drives, and messaging platforms with inconsistent names and no lifecycle logic.

## Intervention

Acrilus-style category structure, naming conventions, and archive separation were introduced.

## Result

Document retrieval became more predictable, backup planning improved, and staff no longer had to guess how to name or place common files.
